# RealityHD 2.0

Scope and research notes for the 2.0 release. Status column is updated as work merges.

## What photoreal outdoor content needs

Scenes read as photographs when four layers hold up at eye height (1.5 m) and at 30 m: the ground
surface, the ground cover sitting on it, the mid layer (rocks, logs, shrubs, props) and the tree canopy.
1.x covers each layer with one or two items. A viewer in an ImmersiveSpace notices repetition first,
then flat cards in grass, then uniform ground, then CAD-clean props. 2.0 attacks them in that order.

Reference practice from production vegetation and terrain tools (SpeedTree, Houdini terrain, Quixel
Megascans scan libraries, Unreal PCG foliage) that procedural generation can match:

| Area | Practice | 2.0 work |
|---|---|---|
| Trees | Species-specific skeletons, branch collars, root flare, per-level bark scale, leaf clusters at twig tips, hierarchical wind (trunk sway, branch bend, leaf flutter) | TreeGenerator v2, 6+ broadleaf and 6+ conifer/palm/desert species |
| Grass | Geometry blades near the camera, cards at distance, per-blade color and dead blades, dense mixed species | Geometry grass tufts with card LODs, lawn, tall, dry and seed-head grass |
| Ground cover | Ferns, flowers, clover, litter, mushrooms, moss breaking up the grass | Plants folder: fern, wildflowers, clover, reeds, mushrooms, moss, leaf litter |
| Ground | Scanned-looking albedo, strong height, wet/dry roughness, macro variation, material blending | gravel, sand, mud, snow, cobble, dirt, needles programs; splat blending |
| Rocks | Erosion and strata, sharp fractures with small bevels, moss by slope, sunk contact | Boulder v2, outcrop, cliff, scree, river stones, strata rock |
| Props | Real dimensions, bevels, wear where hands and weather reach | Campsite, Construction and Farm packs |
| Repetition | Per-instance hue, value and scale variation | Instancing tint jitter |
| Engine | Texture caching, transparency, water | Disk texture cache, transparent mode, water |
