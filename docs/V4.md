# RealityHD 4.0

4.0 adds articulated multi-state assets, building interiors, and the engine work that keeps them
cheap: static batching, a scene ambient-occlusion bake, an indoor light probe and shared tint textures.

## Articulation

`Rig` (RealCore/Rig.swift): static base, parts on revolute, prismatic or fixed joints, parent chains,
mimic joints, part options with alternate geometry, lights bound to options, named states.
`RealArticulated` assets get `build(seed:)` (default state, baked) for free. RealKit uploads one
entity per part with the pivot baked out, animates part transforms with an eased tween
(`RealArticulationSystem`) and switches LOD across the whole part hierarchy. Guide:
`docs/guides/articulation.md`.

## Interiors

`Room` (RealLibrary/Building/Room.swift) builds a closed shell: floor, walls with door and window
openings, columns, skirting, lay-in T-bar ceiling or plaster, ceiling void and slab, so the sun enters
only through openings. Inner faces are tessellated for the AO bake. `fixturePattern` leaves grid cells
open for `ceiling-light`.

`InteriorLight` (RealKit/Environment.swift) is a procedural indoor probe (ceiling with luminaires,
walls, floor, window band showing sky) used as the IBL in place of the sky; `.office`, `.warm`,
`.lobby` presets. The skybox and sun stay, seen through the windows. `RealScene.lighting` carries the
sky, probe, fog and sun scale; `RealityHD.environment(for:)` and `realityhd render` apply it.

Scenes: `open-office`, `executive-office`, `office-lobby`, `conference-room`, `office-plaza`.

## Optimization

| Feature | Where | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Static batching | `RealScene.batchStatics` | singles merged per 8 m cell, per material, per LOD layout; glass split off without shadows. `open-office`: 127 entities for 36 desk places, cabinets, room and clutter |
| Scene AO bake | `RealScene.bake`, `AOBake`, `BVH` | per-vertex hemisphere occlusion from every static occluder into `occlusion` of `bake` singles at load; 3 s for `open-office` in release, zero per-frame cost |
| Baked-state instancing | `scene.field(asset, state:)` | rows of chairs, monitors and pedestals as GPU instances of one posed state |
| Tint texture sharing | `RealMaterialCache.textures` | `key:RRGGBB` reuses the base key's normal, roughness, AO and metallic maps and synthesizes a half-resolution albedo. `open-office` texture memory 574 MB to 308 MB |
| Rig runtime | `RealArticulationSystem` | one Transform write per moving part per frame; settled joints are skipped |
| Display material | `screen.ui` | unlit textured emitter (no lighting cost) |

## CLI

`realityhd states <id>` renders every state through the live rig. `render --state <name>` bakes one
state. `render` follows a scene's lighting hints unless `--sky` is given. `REALITYHD_TEX_REPORT=1`
lists texture sets per render. CATALOG.md has an Articulated section (states, joints, range).

## Demo

Five new cases. Interiors and the plaza use their own sun and probe. Tapping a part of a live
articulated asset toggles it (`realToggle()`).
